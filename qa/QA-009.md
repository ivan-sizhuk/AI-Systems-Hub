## 1. Scope of change under test

v27.5 changes **exactly one node** — `Return Customer Lookup` (code) — replacing a
timezone-naive `new Date(stored_at)` with a `parseStoredAtMs()` helper that reuses
the `Filter Old Sessions` zone-aware luxon parse, applied to the newest-session sort
and the 2-hour freshness gate. No sibling node, connection, setting, sheet schema,
prompt, or integration changed (verified: only `Return Customer Lookup` differs
v27.4→v27.5). QA therefore centers on the `lookup_customer` path and its end-to-end
use, plus regression assurance that nothing else moved.

## 2. Test approach & environments

Two layers:

- **Automated (logic-level), executed in this QA pass.** The fixed resolution logic
  (parser + session-selection + freshness gate + fallback + customer match) is
  exercised deterministically with synthetic Sessions/Customers/trigger inputs,
  under **both** `TZ=UTC` (n8n default) and `TZ=America/Toronto`. These are

## 4. Multi-workflow / end-to-end impact analysis (Task item 3)

**Architecture confirmed from `workflow-v27.5.json`:** this is a **self-invoking
workflow**. Every tool (`get_availability`, `book_appointment`,
`reschedule_appointment`, `cancel_appointment`, `lookup_customer`,
`lookup_appointment`, `human_handoff`, `end_call`, `estimate_job_ballpark`) is a
`toolWorkflow` node calling the same workflow (`workflowId = {{ $workflow.id }}`).
Entry is `When Executed by Another Workflow → Business Config → Switch` (on
`$json.action`), which routes to per-action branches.

- `Return Customer Lookup` sits **only on the `lookup_customer` (customer-lookup)
  branch** (`Switch → Read Customers - Lookup Customer → Read Session Phone →
  Return Customer Lookup`).
- It is therefore not directly on the booking / reschedule / cancel branches — those
  branches have their **own, unchanged** session-phone resolution
  (`Code2`/`Set Data1`, `Code4`/`Set Data2`, `Cancel Appointment Context`).
- **But** `lookup_customer` is invoked by the agent across booking, reschedule, and
  cancel **conversations** to identify a returning customer, so the fix's effect
  surfaces end-to-end in all three (hence B2–B4).

**Additional end-to-end scenarios that SHOULD be tested (beyond unit):**

1. **B2–B4** — returning-customer identification within booking, reschedule, and
   cancel conversations (the fix's real-world surface across flows).
2. **Non-interference regression** — confirm the unchanged sibling resolution
   (`Code2`/`Code4`/`Cancel Appointment Context`) still resolves the phone as before;
   the fix must not change their behavior (they were not edited, but E2E proves no
   integration coupling).
3. **`lookup_appointment` smoke** — a **different** branch/node
   (`Get Appointment Events - Lookup` / `Return Appointment Lookup`), **not** touched
   by this fix; a smoke check confirms no accidental cross-branch impact.
4. **Concurrency (A8/live)** — two overlapping calls; confirm the known unscoped
   selection does not cross phones in practice (bounded by the Medium finding).
