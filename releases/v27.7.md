## Summary
Extends the `Estimate Job Ballpark` diagnostic-dominant classifier so undiagnosed symptoms across all symptom families route to a diagnostic instead of a priced repair, while explicitly named/requested services stay priced. Reframes the diagnostic message as the first step toward a repair estimate, communicates the diagnostic-fee waiver on repair, and encourages booking — with no diagnostic price quoted.

## Root cause (INV-013)
V27.6 gate `diagnosticOnly = (leakDominant || transmissionSymptom) || (isVagueSymptom && !hasNamedService)` covered only a narrow symptom vocabulary + leak + literal "transmission". Every other symptom family fell through to `hasNamedService`/`matchService`, producing a specific repair price (12/32 symptom inputs) or a `$0` book-a-visit (19/32).

## Change (one node, two regions)
1. **Gate:** `diagnosticOnly = isSymptom && !explicitNamedRequest`. `isSymptom` = leak/drip/puddle OR transmission-complaint OR a broad symptom lexicon OR the existing `isVagueSymptom`. `explicitNamedRequest` = an acquisition/replacement verb (replace/install/swap/new/need/want/get/put in/looking for) applied to a named catalog component.
2. **Diagnostic message:** reworded to the first-step / fee-waiver / booking framing; `startingAtPrice` and `startingAtText` blanked so no diagnostic price is emitted.

## Scope
Exactly 1 node changed. No prompt (`prompt-v28`), catalog/Services Sheet, `matchService`, booking, availability, appointment tools, Twilio, calendar, MCP, or other node/setting changed. Self-diagnosed named-service policy preserved.

## Regression (logic simulation)
118-case two-sided matrix + 32-input symptom sweep:
- **Side A** (no invented prices for symptoms): **0** specific repair prices (was 12 in V27.6); 31/32 → diagnostic; 1 adversarial idiom ("car sounds like a diesel") → `$0` book-a-visit.
- **Side B** (no legit named service turned diagnostic): **0/59** catalog services mis-routed; 0 explicit/mixed/carve-out requests diagnosed.
- **Self-diagnosis preserved:** bare guesses ("probably a ball joint", "I think it's the starter", "pretty sure I need brake pads") stay priced.
- **Message:** fee-waiver present, booking CTA present, no `$` price.
