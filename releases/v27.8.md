## Summary
Extends the BUG-013 diagnostic-dominant symptom lexicon to catch natural-language symptom variants (contractions, specific warning lights, electrical/vibration/overheating/pulling synonyms, no-start/battery idioms) that the live V27.7 QA proved still produced invented repair prices. Classifier-only; the decision rule, named-service/self-diagnosis policy, leak/transmission carve-outs, and the diagnostic customer-facing message are all unchanged.

## Change (one node, two regex lines)
`Estimate Job Ballpark`:
1. `symptomKeywords` extended with: negation/contraction + cold/cool (AC not/isn't/stopped cooling); specific warning lights (`abs|brake|oil|battery|…` + `light|lamp|indicator`) and "light is on/flashing"; electrical failures (`stopped working|quit working|power window/lock/seat/mirror|flicker`); vibration synonym `shimmy`; no-start idiom `turn over`; overheating phrasings (`temp… gauge/red/high/climbing`, `running hot`, `in the red`); pulling synonyms `drift`/`wander`; battery/no-start `needs a jump`; and `isn't/doesn't driving/running right` + `isn't/aren't working`.

Decision rule `diagnosticOnly = isSymptom && !explicitNamedRequest` unchanged. Diagnostic-branch message unchanged (byte-identical to V27.7).
