## Summary
Adds a corroboration guard (BUG-014 Design D) so a weak/ambiguous matcher result can no longer produce a specific repair price for an undiagnosed symptom. A matched repair is priced only if the caller explicitly requested the named service or actually said a surface form of the matched service's part word; otherwise it routes to a diagnostic. `matchService` scoring, the diagnostic message, the self-diagnosis policy, and prompt-v29 are all unchanged.

## Baseline
Generated from the verified **live V27.8** (name "(V27.8)", active, 132 nodes, `availableInMCP=true`, `executionOrder=v1`; deployed EJB byte-identical to repo V27.8). V27.9 preserves `availableInMCP=true`, `executionOrder=v1`, 132 nodes, connections, and all unrelated params.

## Root cause (INV-014)
A single search token that stem-collapses into a service key word ("start" → "starter") scored ≥ 2 and priced a repair. Symptom and request produce identical scores, so min-score/min-margin guards are structurally incapable — corroboration is required.

## Change (one node)
`Estimate Job Ballpark`: at the `diagnosticOnly` decision, compute the match first, then require corroboration:
`priceable = explicitNamedRequest || surfacePresent(matchedPartWord)`; a weak, unrequested match routes to the existing diagnostic branch. `matchService` unchanged.

## Scope
Exactly 1 node changed. No prompt-v29, catalog/Sheet, matcher scoring, booking, availability, tools, Twilio, calendar, MCP, or other node/setting changed. Self-diagnosis and mixed-request behavior preserved.

## Scope of the guarantee (honest)
Meets: "an undiagnosed symptom must not receive a specific repair price **because of a weak or ambiguous service match**." A symptom that explicitly names its own component remains the symptom classifier's domain (currently handled) — the unqualified "NEVER" is not claimed.
