# BUG-005

**Bug ID:** BUG-005
**Title:** Successful bookings are recorded as `info_only`
**Severity:** Medium (provisional — confirm during investigation)
**Status:** Open
**Date:** 2026-07-28 (added to tracker). Original report date: Unknown — not recorded in repository.

---

## Description

A call in which a booking succeeded is recorded in `Call_Records` with an
`Outcome` of `info_only` rather than `booked`.

## Business Impact

Monitoring under-counts successful bookings, distorting booking-rate and
abandonment metrics (`monitoring/METRICS.md`) and undermining the per-call
instrumentation's purpose. Not customer-facing.

## Expected Behaviour

Per [`docs/data-model/call-records.md`](../docs/data-model/call-records.md) and
[`tests/instrumentation.md`](../tests/instrumentation.md): `Outcome` is provided
by the platform when configured, otherwise derived from tools fired —
`book_appointment` → `booked`, `reschedule_appointment` → `rescheduled`,
`cancel_appointment` → `cancelled`, `human_handoff` → `handoff`,
`estimate_job_ballpark` alone → `estimate_only`, otherwise `info_only`, no tool
data → `unknown`. A call where `book_appointment` fired should record `booked`.

## Actual Behaviour

Reported: a call where `book_appointment` succeeded records `Outcome` of
`info_only`. Not independently reproduced in this documentation pass —
reproduction and confirmation are pending investigation.

## Steps to Reproduce

Unknown — requires investigation. Proposed starting point (not yet executed):

`Tools Fired` and `Outcome` columns to see whether `book_appointment` was detected
