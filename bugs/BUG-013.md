## Description
After BUG-012 (leak/transmission), the V27.6 `Estimate Job Ballpark` classifier still routed most undiagnosed symptoms to a priced repair. The diagnostic gate covered only a narrow `symptomOnlyKeywords` list plus leak and the literal word "transmission"; every other symptom family fell through to `hasNamedService` / `matchService` and produced either a specific (often wrong) repair price or a `$0` book-a-visit fallback.

## Business Impact
The agent quoted specific, sometimes expensive, sometimes wrong repairs for undiagnosed symptoms and offered to book them — e.g. "car won't start" → starter $549, "AC not blowing cold" → a/c compressor $899, "steering wheel shakes when I brake" → wheel alignment $129, "burning oil smell" → oil pan gasket $399. This violates `prompt-v28` (lines 62-64: symptoms → diagnostic, no invented pricing).

## Expected Behaviour
An undiagnosed symptom/problem routes to a diagnostic; a clearly named, explicitly requested service stays priced. The diagnostic is framed as the first step toward a repair estimate; no repair price is invented from symptoms.

## Actual Behaviour (V27.6)
32-input symptom sweep: 12 produced a specific invented repair price, 19 produced a `$0` book-a-visit, 1 routed to diagnostic.

## Investigation Status
Confirmed — see `investigations/INV-013.md`.

## Root Cause
`diagnosticOnly = (leakDominant || transmissionSymptom) || (isVagueSymptom && !hasNamedService)`. The symptom vocabulary was too narrow; any component word or scored-matcher token match produced a priced repair.

## Fix (V27.7)
`diagnosticOnly = isSymptom && !explicitNamedRequest`, where `isSymptom` covers the full symptom-family lexicon (noises, vibration, no-start, rough-run/misfire, overheating, smells/smoke, electrical/warning-lights, AC-not-cold, pulling, battery-dying, loose/soft feel, hard-shift/slip, leak/drip/puddle, vague "something wrong"), and `explicitNamedRequest` (acquisition verb + named catalog component) preserves named-service pricing and the leak/transmission carve-outs. The diagnostic-branch message was reworded to frame the diagnostic as the first step, communicate the fee waiver on repair, and encourage booking — with no diagnostic price quoted.

## Scope
Only the `Estimate Job Ballpark` code node changed (gate logic + diagnostic message). No prompt, catalog/Sheet, matcher, booking, availability, tools, Twilio, calendar, MCP, or other nodes/settings changed. Self-diagnosed named-service policy preserved.

## Files Involved
Live node `Estimate Job Ballpark` (V27.6 → V27.7). Fix artifact: `production/workflow-v27.7.json`.
