## Description
For undiagnosed leaks (e.g., "my coolant is leaking") and undiagnosed transmission complaints (e.g., "my transmission is acting up"), `Estimate Job Ballpark` returned `diagnosticOnly=false` and a repair price instead of recommending a diagnostic.

## Business Impact
The agent can quote a specific — and often wrong — repair price for an undiagnosed symptom: e.g., a $199 transmission fluid change for a transmission complaint, or a $549 radiator / $149 coolant flush for a coolant leak.

## Expected Behaviour
`tests/diagnostics.md` Must Never: "Invent a repair price for an undiagnosed symptom" and "Skip the diagnostic recommendation for vague symptoms (noises, leaks, overheating, electrical, transmission concerns)." Undiagnosed leaks/transmission complaints route to a diagnostic; clearly-named services stay priced (named-service override).

## Actual Behaviour
`diagnosticOnly=false` + a repair price for undiagnosed leaks/transmission complaints. Confirmed by executing the live node against the live Services catalog.

## Steps to Reproduce
Call `estimate_job_ballpark` with `service="my coolant is leaking"` (or `"transmission is acting up"`) → returns a priced repair, not a diagnostic.

## Investigation Status
Confirmed — see `investigations/INV-012.md`.

## Root Cause
`Estimate Job Ballpark`: `diagnosticOnly = isVagueSymptom && !hasNamedService`. "leak" absent from `symptomOnlyKeywords`; components co-occurring with leaks are in `namedServiceKeywords` (forcing `diagnosticOnly=false`); bare "transmission" in neither set.

## Files Involved
Live workflow node `Estimate Job Ballpark` (V27.5 → fixed in V27.6). Fix artifact: `production/workflow-v27.6.json`.
