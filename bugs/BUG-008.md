## Closure summary

**Status: Closed.**

**Reason:** Originally created as an engineering investigation into transactional
integrity — whether booking, rescheduling, and cancellation behave as a true
transaction across Google Calendar, the Appointments sheet, the Customers sheet,
Call Records, and internal workflow state, and whether a partial failure could
leave data inconsistent.

After additional testing, the issue could not be reproduced. **No confirmed defect
currently exists.** This entry is retained as historical documentation of the
investigation and its outcome; it is listed under **Closed Investigations** in
`INDEX.md`, not among Active Bugs, and is not reopened unless new evidence
reintroduces the concern.

## Description

This was an **investigation, not a confirmed defect**. Its purpose was to
determine whether a partial failure in a scheduling operation could leave one
system updated while another was not — for example: Calendar updated while the
Sheets are not; orphaned Calendar events; orphaned Appointment rows; mismatched
Appointment IDs; incorrect Customer records; incorrect Call Records.

## Business Impact

Potential (unconfirmed) impact considered during the investigation: silent data
inconsistency across Calendar/Sheets and, in the worst hypothesized case, a
success reported to the customer or to monitoring when an operation had only
partially completed. No such defect was confirmed, so no realized impact is
attributed.

## Expected Behaviour (the property that was under test)

A booking, reschedule, or cancellation should leave every system it touches
mutually consistent, or leave them all unchanged. Relevant documented intent:
[`SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md`](../SYSTEM_CONTRACT.md) Failure Contracts;
[`docs/workflows/cancellation.md`](../docs/workflows/cancellation.md) Error
Handling ("the appointment should remain unchanged. No partial updates should
occur."); [`DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md) create-before-delete rescheduling and
Appointments-record-as-source-of-truth.

## Actual Behaviour

Not reproducible. Additional testing did not surface a transactional-integrity
failure; no confirmed defect exists at closure.

## Steps to Reproduce

No reproduction was established. (Investigation approach considered: enumerate the
ordered Calendar/Appointments/Customers/Call-Records writes per scheduling flow in
the deployed artifact, simulate a failure of each downstream write, and observe
whether any inconsistent end-state or false success could result.)

## Investigation Status

Closed. Concluded without a confirmed defect.

## Root Cause

Not applicable — no confirmed defect. Recorded historical note: the documented
cancellation design treats Appointments-sheet and SMS updates as non-blocking
after the calendar delete (`docs/tools/cancel_appointment.md`), which was the
primary characteristic examined; testing did not reproduce a resulting
inconsistency.

## Acceptance Criteria

Closed as not-reproducible; no fix acceptance criteria apply. If the concern
recurs, reopen via a new bug report with reproduction evidence and confirm the
end-state of every system under the failing condition.
